# Code of Conduct

The p2a organization adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies to every repository, issue, pull request, and discussion in [p2a-sh](https://github.com/p2a-sh), and to anyone representing p2a in public spaces.

## In short

- Be respectful and welcoming, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, or personal attacks.
- Don't publish anyone's private information without their explicit permission.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **info@p2a.sh**. Every report will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and we will respect the reporter's privacy. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, or ban contributors, temporarily or permanently, as set out in the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
